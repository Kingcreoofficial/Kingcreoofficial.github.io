HOW TO PREPARE

Ready in 20 Minutes From Freezer to Table.

1. KEEP FROZEN — Store in freezer until ready to use.

2. HEAT — Empty contents into a saucepan. Heat on medium.

3. STIR & SIMMER — Stir occasionally and simmer for 15–20 minutes.

4. SERVE & ENJOY — Pour over your favorite rice or noodles. Serve and enjoy!
